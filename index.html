Hussain Awan

Lawyer Teaching Fellow Mentor

Read more on Hussain’s Harvard University page: winthrop.harvard.edu/people/hussain-awan

Hussain Awan is a recent graduate of Harvard Law School. He has written on legislative retroactivity, litigation, democratization, religious legal systems, and anticorruption. He is a contributor to Harvard Law School's Global Anticorruption Blog, a Harvard Law School Chayes Fellow, and a former volunteer clerk to Justice Syed Mansoor Ali Shah at the Supreme Court of Pakistan. Awan attended McGill University in Montreal, where he studied International Development and French and graduated as class valedictorian.
